# Inpatient Sepsis Screening & Escalation Protocol

> **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE PROTOCOL** — authored to exercise the Guideline Copilot pipeline.
> Not medical advice. Not for clinical use.

- Corpus ID: SEPSIS-SCR | Edition: ed.2 | Effective: 2026-01-15
- Owner (synthetic): Clinical Quality & Patient Safety Committee

## 1. Purpose

Standardise bedside screening for sepsis on all adult inpatient wards so that
deterioration is detected early, escalated quickly, and treated within the first-hour
bundle targets. Screening is everyone's job: nursing, responding teams, and ward
pharmacists all participate.

## 2. Screening criteria

Screen **every inpatient at least once per nursing shift** and whenever a concern is
raised. A screen is positive when either:

- **Two or more SIRS criteria** are present together with clinical suspicion of
  infection: temperature > 38.3 °C or < 36 °C; heart rate > 90 bpm; respiratory rate
  > 20; white cell count > 12 or < 4 ×10⁹/L; new altered mentation.
- **qSOFA score ≥ 2:** respiratory rate ≥ 22/min, systolic blood pressure ≤ 100 mmHg,
  or new altered mentation.

## 3. Escalation pathway

On a positive screen: notify the responding team **within 15 minutes**, and obtain
**senior clinical review within 30 minutes** of the positive screen. The reviewing
clinician documents whether sepsis is confirmed, possible, or excluded, and whether
the first-hour bundle is activated. If the patient is on a ward without resident
senior cover, escalate to the on-call medical registrar immediately.

## 4. First-hour bundle

When sepsis is confirmed or strongly suspected, complete within 60 minutes:

- Measure lactate; **repeat within 2 hours if the initial lactate is ≥ 2 mmol/L**.
- Take **two sets of blood cultures before antibiotics** (unless this delays
  antibiotics by more than 45 minutes).
- Administer **broad-spectrum antibiotics within 60 minutes** of the positive screen.
- Begin **30 mL/kg crystalloid** for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
- Apply supplemental oxygen to target saturation per institutional policy.

## 5. Documentation

Record the screening result, escalation times, bundle element completion times, and
the review outcome on the sepsis screening form. Times are audited monthly by the
Clinical Quality Committee; ward-level feedback is returned to nurse managers.
