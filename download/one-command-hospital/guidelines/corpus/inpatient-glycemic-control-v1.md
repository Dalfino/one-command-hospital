# Inpatient Glycemic Control Protocol

> **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE PROTOCOL** — authored to exercise the Guideline Copilot pipeline.
> Not medical advice. Not for clinical use.

- Corpus ID: GLYCEMIC-CTRL | Edition: v1.0 | Effective: 2026-05-01
- Owner (synthetic): Diabetes & Endocrinology Governance Group

## 1. Scope

Applies to adults with diabetes or stress hyperglycemia admitted to general medical and
surgical wards. It does not cover critical care (separate ICU protocol), diabetic
ketoacidosis or hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state (separate emergency pathways), paediatric
patients, or pregnancy outside the peri-partum target note in Section 2.

## 2. Glycemic targets

- **Most inpatients:** capillary blood glucose (CBG) **7.8–10.0 mmol/L**.
- **Relaxed target (10.0–13.9 mmol/L):** frail patients, those at the end of life, and
  patients with impaired hypoglycemia awareness, where avoiding hypoglycemia outweighs
  tight control.
- **Stricter target (6.1–7.8 mmol/L)** applies only peri-partum, with endocrinology input.

## 3. Monitoring

- **Non-insulin treatment:** CBG before meals and at bedtime (minimum every 6 hours).
- **Insulin-treated:** a minimum of **four measurements per day** (pre-meals and bedtime).
- **Insulin infusion:** check **hourly** until stable, then **1–2 hourly**.
- **Ketones:** check when CBG exceeds **14.0 mmol/L**, and for any type 1 diabetes patient
  with intercurrent illness.

## 4. Hypoglycemia management

Hypoglycemia is defined as **CBG below 4.0 mmol/L**.

- **Conscious and able to swallow:** give **15 g of fast-acting carbohydrate** (the rule
  of 15); **recheck CBG after 15 minutes**; repeat treatment until CBG is at least
  4.0 mmol/L, then give a long-acting carbohydrate snack.
- **Reduced consciousness or unable to swallow:** give **80 mL of 20% glucose IV**, or
  **1 mg glucagon IM** if IV access is unavailable; recheck in 15 minutes.
- **Documentation:** record every episode on the **hypoglycemia chart** and review the
  cause the same day.

## 5. Escalation and insulin adjustment

- **Two or more hypoglycemia episodes in 24 hours:** contact the **diabetes specialist
  nurse** during working hours, or the **on-call medical registrar** out of hours.
- Consider an **insulin infusion** for type 1 diabetes patients unable to eat, peri-operative
  patients with type 1 diabetes, or persistent CBG above 15.0 mmol/L on twice-daily or
  basal-bolus regimens.
- Recurring severe derangement is discussed with endocrinology before regimen changes.

## 6. Peri-operative fasting

- Once-daily long-acting insulin: continue at **80% of the usual dose** on fasting days.
- **Metformin: omit on the day of surgery.**
- **SGLT2 inhibitors: omit on the day of surgery and hold for 3 days afterwards**
  (euglycemic ketoacidosis risk).
- During fasting, target CBG 6.0–10.0 mmol/L; use a variable-rate insulin infusion if
  the target is not met orally.
